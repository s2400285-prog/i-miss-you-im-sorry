My apology

I'm Truly Sorry, Baby Julie.

I’m sorry for the things I said and did that hurt you. Asking you for private photos was unfair to you. I can see how my words may have made you uncomfortable or upset. What I ask of you is my responsibility, and you deserve to feel safe telling me how you feel.

I don’t want you to feel pressured, ignored, or taken for granted. When you say no, I’ll accept your answer the first time. When you want to tell me something difficult, I’ll listen and let you finish. I want to practice the patience, respect, and honesty that loving you calls for.

You can take the time you need, palangga. You don’t owe me an answer to this letter. I want the way I treat you, even on an ordinary day, to show you that I heard you.

A Letter From My Heart.

My dearest Baby Julie,

I thank God that our paths met. Sometimes I have a little piece of news, nothing big at all, and my first thought is that I want to tell you. I love that about you and me. Even a few minutes of talking can become the part of my day I remember.

When we’re apart, I miss hearing the way you tell a story. I miss the little pause before you laugh. Salamat gid, palangga, for sharing pieces of your day with me. The things that seem small to you mean so much to me.

I’m still learning how to love you well. I want to be someone you can laugh with on a good day and speak honestly to on a hard one. I want you to feel loved, respected, safe, and appreciated when you’re with me. I choose you on both kinds of days.

I hope there are many more ordinary afternoons ahead of us, with time to talk, to listen, and to make memories we’ll smile about later. Palangga ta ka, Julie.

With all my love,
James

One Last Thing, Baby.

I hope that through my actions, I can show you how much you mean to me. I don't just want to say that I love you. I want to keep learning how to love you better, every single day. I love you, Baby Julie.
