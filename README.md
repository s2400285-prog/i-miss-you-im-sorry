# A Letter From My Heart
A personal love letter from James Traigo to Baby Julie.

## Add your photo and song
Replace the two empty placeholder files with your own media before giving Julie the letter. Keep these exact names (lowercase) and file types.

| Your file | Location |
| --- | --- |
| One JPEG photograph | `assets/images/us.jpg` |
| Your MP3 song | `assets/music/mysong.mp3` |

The frame fits the entire photo without cropping or filters. Until a photo is supplied, it shows an illustrated placeholder. The music menu can also preview a song from the device for the current visit.

## Open the website
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open the **I miss you Im sorry** folder and double-click **index.html** to open it in your browser. Keep the **js**, **css**, and **assets** folders beside it.
3. Select **Open My Letter**.

You can also open the folder in VS Code and select **Open with Live Server** on **index.html**. Refresh the page after replacing your photo or song.

## Reading the letter
The envelope opens into the photo, apology, love letter and closing note. The chapter controls can open any section. A normal page turn takes five seconds. Select **Skip transition** or press **Escape** to finish immediately. Reduced-motion readers move directly to the next section.

Opening the letter starts the supplied song at 32% volume where the browser permits it. If playback is blocked, select **Play Our Song**. The song menu has play/pause and mute controls. The same audio element stays mounted across sections and replays of the letter. Some mobile browsers use the device volume buttons to control loudness.

Edit messages in **index.html**, design in **css/style.css**, and interactions in **js/script.js**. Lucide SVG icons and the Allura, Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans fonts are bundled with their licenses in **assets**.
